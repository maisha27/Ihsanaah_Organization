Session Structure

01

Book Discussion

  • Introduction to the new month's book
  • Discussion of the previous book — reader's reflections, author's intent, personal connections & Islamic themes
02

Creative Journaling & Gameplay

  • Reading Journal Personal reflections, quotes, doodles, calligraphy, and decorative elements for each completed book
  • Gameplay Informative, intellectual, and bonding games — Code Names, Book Bingo, 5 Pillars of Islam, The Game
