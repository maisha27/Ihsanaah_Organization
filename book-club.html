What We're About
A safe, inspiring space to explore books with deep Islamic themes, engage in meaningful discussions, and grow spiritually, intellectually, and creatively.
— Verses & Vibes Mission

The club aims to nurture sisterhood, self-expression, and a love for books among young Muslimahs — serving as an educational, faith-centred form of entertainment.

Our Vision

"A vibrant space where young Muslimahs build positive connections, deepen their understanding of Islam, and express themselves authentically."

Membership Includes

What We Provide

2 Sessions / Month

Structured, regular gatherings to keep the rhythm of reading alive.

Reflective Reading Sheets

Guided prompts to help members engage deeply with each book.

Creative Journaling

Guided journaling sessions combining writing, calligraphy, and doodles.

Author Sessions

Special conversations with authors — virtually or in-person when available.

Cosy Space & Snacks

A welcoming discussion environment with snacks for offline members.

Strong Sisterhood

A community of 15–20 committed offline members, plus unlimited online.

Each Month

Session Structure

01

Book Discussion

  • → Introduction to the new book
  • → Discussion of the previous book — reader's reflections, author's intent, personal connections & Islamic themes
02

Creative Journaling & Gameplay

  • Reading Journal

    Each member keeps a personal journal — written reflections, quotes, doodles, calligraphy, and decorative elements for each completed book.

  • Gameplay

    Informative, intellectual, and bonding games — non-competitive. Think Code Names, Book Bingo, 5 Pillars of Islam, The Game.

Special Segment — Conversation with the Author

When available, the author joins virtually or in-person to share their story, insights, and intentions — followed by a live Q&A with club members.
