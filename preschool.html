Our Philosophy
Every child carries a fitra — a pure, natural inclination towards goodness and the Divine. Our role is simply to nurture what Allah ﷻ has already placed within them.

Little Mumin Islamic Preschool introduces children to faith, character, and learning through age-appropriate activities in a loving and safe space. We believe the earliest years are the most formative — and we take that responsibility with care.

Kids Offline Maktab

What children learn

Our in-person Maktab programme gives children a strong Islamic foundation from a young age — in a structured, caring environment.

01

Kids Hifz

Beginning Quran memorisation tailored for young learners — structured progressively so children build confidence with each juz.

02

Nazeera

Correct Quran recitation with proper pronunciation — so children learn to read beautifully and confidently from the very beginning.

03

Basic to Advanced Tajweed

Age-appropriate Tajweed rules introduced gently, helping children read the Quran with care and beauty at every level.

04

Basic Islam Learning

Foundational Islamic education — pillars of faith, everyday duas, Islamic character, and the etiquettes of a Muslim life.
