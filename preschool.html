Our Philosophy
Every child carries a fitra — a pure, natural inclination towards goodness and the Divine. Our role is simply to nurture what Allah ﷻ has already placed within them.

Little Mumin Islamic Preschool introduces children to faith, character, and learning through age-appropriate activities in a loving and safe space. We believe the earliest years are the most formative — and we take that responsibility with immense care.
