About Ihsaanah
We believe education is an act of worship, and every learner a trust.

Ihsaanah is an Islamic education and empowerment initiative dedicated to nurturing individuals who strive for excellence in both this life and the Hereafter. Rooted in the values of Iman, Ihsaan, knowledge, character, and service, Ihsaanah creates meaningful learning experiences for children, women, families, and the wider community.

Our approach to Islamic education goes beyond knowledge alone — we seek to cultivate faith, character, competence, and a sense of responsibility to serve. Every course, programme, and gathering is designed with this purpose at its heart.

Based in Mirpur-2, Dhaka, Ihsaanah has grown into a home for those who desire learning with sincerity — where the Quran is taught with care, entrepreneurship is encouraged through an Islamic lens, and the youngest hearts are nurtured from their earliest years.

Our Mission

Faith.
Excellence.
Service.

Al Ihsaanah exists to nurture and empower Muslim women, children, and families through holistic education rooted in the Qur'an and Sunnah. We integrate Islamic values with academic excellence, life skills, leadership, entrepreneurship, and community service — developing individuals who worship Allah ﷻ with Ihsan, live with purpose and integrity, and contribute meaningfully to society.
