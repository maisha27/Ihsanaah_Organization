Programme 01

Little Mumin Kidpreneurs

Discover Create Serve Lead

An entrepreneurship and leadership programme for young Muslims — encouraging children to discover their talents, create with purpose, serve their community, and grow into confident leaders grounded in Islamic values.

Events include market days where young participants set up stalls, sell their creations, visit and encourage one another, and practise generosity and enterprise as acts of worship.

Programme Pillars

  1. 01 Discover your gifts
  2. 02 Create with purpose
  3. 03 Serve the community
  4. 04 Lead with integrity
